# Name and branding

This project does **not** assert trademark rights over the name “Docuvate” or the Docuvate logo. This file states **naming and branding policy** only, not legal ownership claims.

## Policy

- Do **not** use the name **Docuvate** or the Docuvate logo for a fork, derivative, or redistribution in a way that suggests it is the official project, an official release channel, or endorsed by Thomas Faust or the Docuvate maintainers.
- Forks and redistributions of Community Edition source must use a **distinct product name** and their own branding.
- Factual phrases such as “based on Docuvate” are acceptable only together with a clear statement that the product is **independent** and **not affiliated**.

Software licensing terms are in [LICENSE](./LICENSE).
