# Support

## Community edition (AGPL)

- [Documentation](docs/)
- [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Docuvate/docuvate/issues) for bugs, feature requests, and questions

There is no guaranteed response time for community support.

## Commercial editions

Team and Business editions include vendor support channels. See [docuvate.de](https://docuvate.de) for licensing and support options.

## Security

Do not use this channel for vulnerabilities. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
